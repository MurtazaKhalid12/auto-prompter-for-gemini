Privacy Policy

Auto-Prompter for Gemini · Last updated 9 October 2026

Auto-Prompter for Gemini ("the extension") is a browser extension made by Murtaza Khalid. This policy explains what data the extension handles. In short: it does not collect, sell or send your data anywhere.

What the extension stores

To work, the extension saves the following only on your own computer, using your browser's local extension storage:

  • Prompts you type into the extension or load from a .txt file
  • Your settings: prompt source, loop mode, delay, alert preferences
  • Run status: which prompt is next, how many were sent, and the last 5 prompts sent with their times

This data never leaves your browser. You can delete it at any time by clicking “Clear” in the extension, or by removing the extension.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect personal information, browsing history, or analytics.
  • It does not read or store Gemini's answers. It only checks the latest reply on the page for a usage-limit message, so it can pause.
  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third-party server.
  • It does not use cookies, trackers or advertising.

Where your prompts go

When you press Start, the extension types your prompts into the Gemini page in your browser, just as you would yourself. What Google does with prompts sent to Gemini is covered by Google's own privacy policy, not this one.

Permissions

  • Access to gemini.google.com — to type prompts into Gemini and show the on-page status.
  • Storage — to save your prompts and settings locally.
  • Notifications — to alert you when automation stops or finishes.
  • Alarms — to check once a minute that automation is still running, so it can alert you if it stalls.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
