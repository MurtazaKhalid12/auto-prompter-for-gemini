Your Gemini prompts,
on autopilot

A free browser extension that sends your list of prompts to Gemini one by one. It waits for each reply, then sends the next on your schedule.

Version 1.0.0 · Works in Brave, Chrome and Edge · No account needed

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for running long prompt lists hands-free, without losing track of what happened.

✎

Three prompt sources

Use the built-in list, upload a .txt file, or write prompts in the extension. Separate prompts with an empty line; a single prompt can span several lines.

∞

Flexible loops

Loop forever, run the list a set number of times, or stop at a time you pick.

⏱

Your pace

Fixed or random delay, in seconds or minutes. Change it while running, or skip a wait.

⏸

Respects limits

If Gemini says you've sent too many prompts, it pauses. It waits longer each time this happens (5 minutes up to 1 hour), then retries the same prompt.

!

Never miss a stop

Get a desktop notification, an on-page alert and a sound if it stops unexpectedly. Resume with one click.

🔒

Private by design

Everything stays in your browser. No accounts, no tracking, no servers.

Install in 2 minutes

The extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store, so you add it in Developer mode. It's safe and takes a few clicks.

  1. Download and unzipDownload the zip, then extract it into a folder you'll keep, for example Documents/auto-prompter.
  2. Open your extensions pageType brave://extensions (Brave), chrome://extensions (Chrome) or edge://extensions (Edge) in the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer modeUse the switch in the top-right corner of the page. In Edge it's in the left sidebar.
  4. Click “Load unpacked”Select the folder you extracted, the one that contains manifest.json.
  5. Pin it and goClick the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Auto-Prompter. Open gemini.google.com, click the icon, and press play.

Updating & good to know

How to update

  • Download the latest zip from this page.
  • Replace the files in your extension folder with the new ones.
  • On the extensions page, click the reload ↻ icon on Auto-Prompter, then refresh your Gemini tab.

Good to know

  • Keep the folder. Deleting it removes the extension.
  • Your browser may show a notice about developer-mode extensions. That's normal for extensions installed this way.
  • Use it responsibly and within Google's terms. Longer delays (30–60s) help you avoid usage limits.
